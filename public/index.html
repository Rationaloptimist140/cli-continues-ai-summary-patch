What the AI summary does

🎯

Goal detection

Reads your session and identifies what you were actually trying to build — not just what commands ran.

✅

Progress snapshot

Lists what was completed this session so the next AI doesn't re-do work or ask obvious questions.

⚡

Decision capture

Surfaces key architectural choices made during the session, so context isn't lost between tools.

🚧

Blocker flagging

Detects failed commands, dead-ends, and unresolved issues and puts them front-and-center.

FAQ

Do I need to provide my own Gemini key?

Yes — you set CONTINUES_GEMINI_KEY in your shell. Your Pro subscription unlocks the feature; Gemini usage is billed to your own Google AI Studio account (generous free tier available).

What happens if I'm offline?

Key validation fails open — if our validation endpoint is unreachable, the AI summary still runs. We never block your workflow due to network issues.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your Stripe customer portal. Your key stays valid until the end of the billing period.

Is the CLI open source?

Yes. The core tool is MIT licensed on GitHub. The Pro gate is the only closed part — it's a thin validation layer, not a rewrite.
